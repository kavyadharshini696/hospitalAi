About This Project

What this is

RxVerify is a Prescription Safety & Ambiguity Detection System — a college prototype, not a diagnostic or prescribing tool. It reads an existing prescription, tells you (and a pharmacist) how confident it is about what it read, and flags anything that looks missing, ambiguous, or inconsistent.

What makes it different from a generic reminder app

  • Confidence-aware prescription interpretation
  • Ambiguous handwriting detection with alternative interpretations
  • Missing-information detection (never auto-filled)
  • Cross-field consistency checking (dose × frequency × duration vs. quantity)
  • Active-ingredient duplicate detection across medicines
  • Human-in-the-loop verification instead of automatic correction
  • Simple-language explanations in English and Tamil

Medical safety notice

This system is an AI-assisted prescription verification tool. It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe medication, change dosage, or replace a doctor or pharmacist. Detected issues must be verified by a qualified healthcare professional.
