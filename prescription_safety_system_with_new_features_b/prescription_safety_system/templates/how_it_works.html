How It Works

1. Upload

A patient uploads a photo, scan, or PDF of a prescription — or pastes the text directly for a reliable demo.

2. Extract

The system preprocesses the image (grayscale, contrast, noise removal), runs OCR, and pulls out structured fields: medicine name, strength, dosage, frequency, duration, quantity, and food instructions. This uses transparent, rule-based pattern matching — not a black box — so every decision is explainable.

3. Analyze

Every field gets a real confidence score from the OCR engine. A rules engine then checks for:

  • Ambiguous handwriting / low-confidence text
  • Missing required fields (dosage, frequency, duration)
  • Duplicate active ingredients across medicines
  • Quantity vs. dose × frequency × duration mismatches
  • Shorthand abbreviations (OD, BD, TDS, etc.) that need confirming

Each issue is scored HIGH MEDIUM LOW and the prescription gets an overall completeness score.

4. Verify

A pharmacist/verifier sees the original prescription and the AI's interpretation side-by-side, and confirms or corrects each uncertain field. The AI never overwrites a doctor's prescription on its own.

5. Download

Once verified, a clean digital prescription PDF is generated, with a plain-language explanation available in English or Tamil.
